## What email.pm.d takes from it

1. **The problem:** sending email is the bottleneck.
2. **The method:** start from the primitives we have (sites we can create, OAuth capabilities on the mailbox, components and UIs, sgit vaults, signatures, the web of trust) and map email workflows onto them. Experiment; keep what works.
3. **Workflows first**, then CRM (Dinis writes on CRM next).
4. **Many designs:** about five very different inboxes and experiences, learning from the best clients out there.
5. **The human stays in the loop:** Dinis reviews; the aim is that agent sessions may also send, step by step, under policy he signs.
6. **Sign everything**, and use the web of trust.
7. **email.pm.d coordinates the email.* sessions**, on a tight schedule of its own, and iterates.
