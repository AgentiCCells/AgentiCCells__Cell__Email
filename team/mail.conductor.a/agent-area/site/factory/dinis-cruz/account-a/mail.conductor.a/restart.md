3. Take every time from `date -u`, git or a message stamp.
4. Check dev and live (/version.json), board/HOLD.md, and the team's branches.
5. Ask a.maestro for anything from other accounts; send_message reaches a.maestro, mail.dev.a, crm.dev.a only.
