What this page does, and does not

  • It sends you to Google's own consent screen. Google returns a token to this page in the address bar's fragment, which browsers never send to a server; the page removes it at once.
  • The token stays in this tab's memory. It is never stored, and the page can connect only to Google's Gmail and token-revocation services: its content security policy forbids anything else.
  • It reads two things: your profile (address, counts, history cursor) and your labels. No message, subject or sender is read, and nothing is written to Gmail, a vault or anywhere else.
  • Disconnect revokes the token at Google. Closing the tab forgets it; it expires within an hour regardless.
  • Next, once you have created your vault through pki.sgit.ai: the page syncs headers into it.
